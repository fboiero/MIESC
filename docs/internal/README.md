# Internal development logs

These documents are **development scaffolding**, not user- or reader-facing
documentation. They record the lane coordination, iteration plans, promotion
audits and pre-camera-ready drafts used while producing the RIBCi–CYTED 2026
papers and the v6.1.0 release. They are kept for project history and traceability.

For the authoritative, reader-facing material use instead:

- Papers: `paper/ribci-cyted-2026/paper{1,2}-camera-ready.pdf`
- Reproducibility: `paper/PAPER1_REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, `paper/PAPER2_REPRODUCIBILITY.md`
- Per-claim evidence: `benchmarks/results/paper{1,2}_claims_matrix.json`
- Claim verification: `python benchmarks/verify_paper_claims.py`
- Known paper↔repo discrepancies: `paper/ribci-cyted-2026/ERRATA.md`

Contents here (lane logs, `*_vnext_*` evidence/templates, promotion/rigor audits,
improvement and review plans, publication/release instructions) may contain
superseded numbers or version-specific notes; do not cite them as current
evidence.
